# 🔬 SciChamp - Interactive Science Learning Games for Students

**SciChamp** is a colorful, playful, and completely free interactive science learning website designed for students, teachers, and curious minds. It features hands-on, playable games across **Chemistry**, **Physics**, and **Biology** with zero login, zero payment, and zero installation required.

---

## 🚀 How to Open & Play

You can open and play **SciChamp** directly in any modern web browser:

1. Navigate to the folder:
   ```
   C:\Users\GKM\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\scichamp
   ```
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to open it in **Google Chrome**, **Microsoft Edge**, **Mozilla Firefox**, or **Safari**.
3. That's it! Everything works 100% offline and standalone with no servers or npm packages needed.

## 🎮 Playable Games Overview

### 🧪 Chemistry Games
1. **Molecule Maker**:
   - Synthesize real molecules like Water ($H_2O$), Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$), Table Salt ($NaCl$), Methane ($CH_4$), Oxygen Gas ($O_2$), and Hydrogen Peroxide ($H_2O_2$).
   - Tap/dispense Hydrogen, Oxygen, Carbon, Sodium, and Chlorine atoms into the bubbling beaker.
   - Verify chemical formulas and observe celebration reactions!
2. **Acid or Base? pH Color Lab**:
   - Test everyday substances (lemon juice, vinegar, milk, pure water, soap, bleach, and baking soda) using Universal Indicator.
   - Watch the test tube liquids change into vibrant colors (Red for acid, Green for neutral, Purple for base).
   - Sort them into their scientific categories and discover real pH levels!

### ⚡ Physics Games
3. **Circuit Master & Conductor Test**:
   - Complete electrical circuits by connecting batteries, alligator clips, and knife switches.
   - Test 8 real-world materials (iron key, gold ring, rubber duck, plastic ruler, silver coin, wooden stick, steel clip, and glass marble) to see if electric current flows to illuminate the glowing bulb.
4. **Gravity Rocket Launch**:
   - Experiment with physics kinematics! Launch a space rocket toward a planetary target base.
   - Adjust the **launch angle** (15° to 85°) and **thruster power** (30% to 100%).
   - Switch planetary gravities:
     - 🌙 **Moon** ($2.2\text{ m/s}^2$) — floaty, high-arcing flight.
     - 🌍 **Earth** ($9.8\text{ m/s}^2$) — standard planetary trajectory.
     - 🪐 **Jupiter** ($24.8\text{ m/s}^2$) — heavy gravitational pull.

### 🌱 Biology Games
5. **Build a Plant Cell**:
   - Explore cellular biology! Place vital organelles into their proper locations inside the cell:
     - **Nucleus** (Command center / DNA storage)
     - **Mitochondria** (Cell powerhouse / ATP energy producer)
     - **Chloroplast** (Photosynthesis / solar food kitchen)
     - **Cell Wall** (Rigid cellulose structural armor)
     - **Cell Membrane** (Selective gatekeeper)
     - **Vacuole** (Water and nutrient storage tank)
6. **Ecosystem Food Chain**:
   - Arrange living organisms into the correct trophic order of energy flow across three biomes:
     - 🌲 **Sunny Forest**: Sun $\rightarrow$ Oak Leaves $\rightarrow$ Caterpillar $\rightarrow$ Bluebird $\rightarrow$ Red-Tailed Hawk
     - 🌊 **Deep Ocean**: Sun $\rightarrow$ Phytoplankton $\rightarrow$ Krill $\rightarrow$ Salmon $\rightarrow$ Great White Shark
     - 🏜️ **Wild Desert**: Sun $\rightarrow$ Cactus $\rightarrow$ Desert Beetle $\rightarrow$ Spotted Lizard $\rightarrow$ Golden Eagle
7. **Human Body Organs Quest**:
   - Drag and drop vital organs (Brain, Heart, Lungs, Stomach, and Skeleton) into their anatomical positions in the human body silhouette.
   - Features animated heart pulsing, breathing lung expansion, neural sparkle animations, and fun physiological facts!

### 🏆 Master Science Trivia Challenge
8. **SciChamp Master Quiz**:
   - Cross-discipline trivia challenge testing students on Chemistry, Physics, and Biology.
   - Includes **50:50 Lifeline**, **streak multipliers (2x, 3x)**, instant visual feedback, and scientific explanations for every question.

## ✨ Features & Polish

- **🔊 Web Audio API Sound Synthesizer**: Custom synthesized clicks, pops, chemical fizzing, sparks, launch sounds, and victory fanfares without external audio files. Includes a sound mute toggle in the navigation header.
- **🎊 Lag-Free Canvas Confetti**: Celebratory particle effects when students solve puzzles or reach milestones.
- **⭐ Star Points & Rank Progression**: Earn Science Stars stored in browser `localStorage`, unlocking titles from *Curious Explorer* to *Science Grand Champion*.
- **💡 Daily Science Fact Ticker**: Interactive rotating facts to spark curiosity.
- **🔍 Quick Search & Category Filters**: Easily filter games by subject or keyword.
- **📱 Responsive & Touch-Friendly**: Works seamlessly with touch taps on iPads, tablets, and smartphones, as well as mouse clicks on desktops.
